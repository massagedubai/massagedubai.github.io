Finding the best massage in Dubai

Dubai is one of the most exciting cities in the world for spa and wellness. Within a short taxi ride you can book a traditional Thai massage, a Kerala Ayurvedic oil treatment, a Moroccan bath, a Russian-style deep massage, a lymphatic drainage session or a luxurious couples massage with sea views. Dubai massage services range from neighbourhood massage centres charging around AED 100 to five-star hotel spas where a single treatment can pass AED 1,000.

That variety is wonderful, but it also makes it hard to know what a fair massage price in Dubai looks like, which massage centre in Dubai is trustworthy, or which style suits your body. MassageDubai is an independent guide written to answer exactly those questions. We explain every major type of massage in Dubai, show typical prices by duration and area, and share practical tips so you can book a safe, professional massage with confidence, whether you are a resident, a tourist or a business traveller.

All prices on this page are indicative ranges in UAE dirhams (AED) for 2026, usually for a 60-minute session, based on published spa menus. Always confirm the final price with the spa before booking.

Home massage service in Dubai

Home massage in Dubai has become hugely popular. A licensed mobile massage company sends a trained therapist to your villa, apartment or hotel room with a portable massage table, clean towels and oils. It is ideal for busy parents, new mothers who want postnatal massage at home, people recovering from injury, and travellers who prefer an in-room massage after a long flight.

A home massage in Dubai usually costs AED 200 to 500 for 60 minutes, and some companies add a small call-out fee for distant areas. Most operate from early morning until late at night, and a few offer 24 hour massage bookings in Dubai Marina, Downtown and Business Bay. Book through an official app or website, check that the company is licensed, and read recent reviews before inviting anyone into your home.

In-room hotel massage

Many hotels in Dubai offer massage in your room through their own spa. Ask at reception rather than responding to leaflets.

Best for couples at home

Some services send two therapists for a side-by-side couples massage in your own living room.

What to prepare

A quiet room with space for a table, a shower nearby and a few minutes to share any health conditions.

Massage deals and cheap massage in Dubai

Everyone loves a good massage offer, and Dubai has plenty of them. Weekday promotions, happy-hour slots before 2 pm, loyalty cards, multi-session packages and voucher websites can reduce prices by 30 to 60 percent. Many spas also run seasonal promotions during Ramadan and the Dubai Summer Surprises.

What about a massage in Dubai for 50 AED? Honest answer: at that price you will usually get a 15 to 20 minute foot, head or chair massage at a mall kiosk, or a first-visit introductory deal. A licensed full body massage rarely costs less than AED 100.

Be careful with suspiciously cheap offers. Business cards left on cars, random messaging app numbers and unverified social media pages often lead to unlicensed venues. Stick to licensed spas and official booking platforms to protect your safety, your money and your privacy.

Is massage legal in Dubai?

Yes. Relaxation and therapeutic massage is completely legal in Dubai when it is provided by a licensed business. Spas and massage centres need a trade licence from Dubai's Department of Economy and Tourism, while therapeutic and medical massage, physiotherapy and some wellness treatments require a Dubai Health Authority (DHA) licence. Sexual services are illegal in the UAE, and venues offering them are regularly closed by the authorities.

How to choose a safe massage centre

  • Check the licence. Legitimate spas display their trade licence and, for therapeutic treatments, DHA-licensed therapists.
  • Read recent reviews. Look for detailed Google Maps reviews with photos, not only star ratings.
  • Look for a clear price list. Professional spas publish their menu and never add surprise charges.
  • Share your health details. Mention pregnancy, injuries, high blood pressure, allergies or skin conditions before you start.
  • Arrive 15 minutes early. Fill in the consultation form and enjoy the sauna or steam room if included.
  • Tip if you are happy. Around 10 to 15 percent is appreciated but never required.

Massage therapist jobs, courses and equipment in Dubai

Massage therapist jobs

Hotels, day spas, physiotherapy clinics and home service companies hire massage therapists all year. Salaries vary widely with experience, qualifications and commission, and hotel roles often include accommodation.

Massage therapist licence

To work in therapeutic massage you generally need recognised training, experience and a DHA licence. Relaxation-only roles in spas follow the employer's trade licence. Check current requirements with the DHA.

Massage courses in Dubai

Training centres offer Swedish, deep tissue, sports, Thai, lymphatic drainage and baby massage courses, from short workshops to certified programmes.

Massage chairs and equipment

Massage chair prices in Dubai range from about AED 3,000 to over AED 25,000. Massage guns, foot massagers, massage tables and massage oils are sold in malls and online.

Massage in Dubai: frequently asked questions

How much does a massage cost in Dubai?

A 60-minute massage costs around AED 120 to 250 at a budget massage centre, AED 250 to 450 at a mid-range spa and AED 500 to 1,500 at a luxury hotel spa. Home massage service usually costs AED 200 to 500 per hour.

Is massage legal in Dubai?

Yes, when provided by a licensed spa, wellness centre or healthcare facility. Therapeutic and medical massage requires a DHA licence. Sexual services are illegal.

Can I find a massage in Dubai for 50 AED?

Only for short treatments such as a 15 to 20 minute foot, chair or head massage, or as an introductory deal. A licensed full body massage rarely costs less than AED 100.

Which area of Dubai has the cheapest massage?

Deira, Al Rigga, Bur Dubai, Karama, Al Nahda, Al Qusais and International City usually have the most affordable massage centres.

Are there 24 hour massage services in Dubai?

Some licensed home and hotel massage companies accept late-night bookings, and a few spas open until midnight or later. Most day spas close between 10 pm and midnight.

Can men and women get a massage in Dubai?

Yes. Many spas are unisex, some are ladies-only or gents-only, and most let you request a therapist of the same gender.

Is massage safe during pregnancy?

Prenatal massage is generally offered from the second trimester by trained therapists. Check with your doctor first, especially for high-risk pregnancies.

Should I tip after a massage in Dubai?

Tipping is optional. Around 10 to 15 percent is appreciated when you are happy with the treatment.
